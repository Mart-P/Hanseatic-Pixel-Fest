A few things visitors ask

Quick answers.

Do I need to speak German?

Nope! You're welcome in any language. Visitor information and many sessions are available in English; the team is happy to help.

Can I come by myself?

Absolutely. The fest is friendly for solo visitors, and the play areas are a great way to meet people.

Is the event suitable for kids?

Yes. The festival is family-friendly, and children under 12 can enter free with a ticket-holding adult.

Can I bring food or a bag?

Small personal bags are welcome. Food and drink options are available at the venue; please check venue guidance before your visit.

What if I need accessibility support?

The venue has step-free access. For specific access needs or accommodations, please contact the organizers before the event.

Where should I stay?

Look around St. Pauli, Sternschanze, or the city center for convenient transit and plenty to explore after the fest.
